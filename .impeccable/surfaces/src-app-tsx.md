# Directory surface

Mode: Operate, with Read for the library and record pages.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Goan field guide that turns a practical question into people, reading, and action. Search is the first usable object on the page.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper, forest green ink, sun-yellow active details, generous upright humanist type, original local photographs. Simple rounded controls; open lists and generous reading space. The user explicitly chose this world, which takes precedence over the concept roll.

STORY: A resident recognises the mission, types a need, and finds sourced contacts and resources together. The website's original organisations and stories give the directory its depth.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A restrained wordmark and four navigation links. A substantial green search area paired with a real Goan photograph. The original mission leads; a large search field and starter chips are immediately usable. An open topic index follows, with the first useful resources visible below.

FORM: User-pinned calm Goan field guide; seed:13ed79d5bc46dc62. Standard web navigation and controls. The signature interaction is the field-guide index becoming a grouped answer of contacts, organisations, and reading as a question is searched. Candidate assignment is superseded by the user's explicit direction, not silently rerolled.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints
No italic or oblique text, no decorative side stripes, no invented facts or contact details. Real search, real links, usable empty states, mobile layouts and keyboard navigation. Original raw records remain separate from the presentation. Contact dates and source freshness are visible.
