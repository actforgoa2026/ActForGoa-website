# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user: React and TypeScript with Vite for a fast, static Vercel preview; Python for reproducible archival and extraction; SQLite and JSON for the source catalogue. Search will combine lexical retrieval, curated intent expansion, and local semantic vectors if a suitable model can be shipped without exposing data or requiring paid services.

## Users
People in Goa looking for practical local help, community organisations, reliable reading, and ways to participate. Representative tasks include reporting illegal dumping, arranging extra waste collection, finding local help, and learning how to act on a community concern.

## Product Purpose
Make ActForGoa's existing knowledge searchable, understandable, and actionable. Preserve its thesis, taglines, About content, source links, and community voice.

## Operating Context
Public browsing on phones and desktop computers. Users may arrive with an urgent practical question or a desire to learn and contribute.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Deep archive of publicly accessible source content, downloadable documents, link graph, and broken-link audit. Searchable directory, reading library, stories, people, and participation paths. Large topic menus lead through a need and optional further choice to useful next actions before related organisations and reading. Five authored practical guides cover farming, animal welfare, environmental protection, reporting local issues, and building; they remain drafts in the source data, with source and verification registers, downloadable guides, and editable templates. Reader-facing copy removes editorial review codes and crawl-date banners, preserves useful practical cautions, and offers detailed audit notes in an optional disclosure after the guide. Original guide and review files remain intact. The homepage mixes the current 533 eligible records in 12-entry batches with automatic loading, pause/manual controls, and history restoration. Source provenance is mandatory. Contact information extracted from old pages is not independently verified. Phone data is retained in the original catalogue and archive but removed from frontend record presentation by the public-record layer. Organisation descriptions use original source paragraphs with an explicit missing-biography fallback. No fabricated organisations, telephone numbers, service availability, testimonials, or claims. No live-site replacement is authorised; GitHub repository creation and a Vercel preview are authorised.

## Brand Commitments
ActForGoa. Original mission and factual source material retained. The current user-approved direction is a restrained wireframe-style menu: warm paper, deep green, upright Manrope only, Lucide outline icons, strong readable hierarchy, and large outlined choices. Home presents eight topics with Waste & recycling first, followed by a topic-to-need-to-next-action flow. The earlier bright yellow/pink palette, hero flowers, and filled illustrations are superseded. Original photography continues in discovery and articles. Shared Arc controls, including SiteHeader, remain binding. Reading and records use open layouts and structural rules. No serif, italic, or oblique type and no decorative side stripes.

## Evidence on Hand
https://actforgoa.org/ and its public WordPress API, sitemaps, HTML, linked resources, and assets. Archived source evidence and derived records live in archive/ and data/. Original story/resource photography is restored from the existing crawl; article-image-map.json records source and rendition provenance, and missing sources remain reported.

## Product Principles
- Start with a topic and a person's need, then show a concrete next action before related resources and contacts.
- Retain source, retrieval date, and verification state in the evidence layer; show useful source context and practical cautions to readers, with detailed audit material available optionally.
- Preserve source material separately from edited presentation.
- Offer search and browsing over the same records.
- Be useful without an account or an external AI service.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, labelled controls, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts are implementation requirements. Large outlined targets, consistent outline icons, and plain visible labels support readers with limited English. Icons supplement text and accessible names; they do not constitute translation. Reading copy stays 18px on phones and desktop. Additional translations remain an open editorial decision. Legal review and live service checks for practical guides remain open even when their internal review codes are absent from the primary reading flow.
