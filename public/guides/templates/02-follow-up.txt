DRAFT TEMPLATE: FOLLOW-UP OR ADMINISTRATIVE ESCALATION
This does not replace a statutory appeal. Check any applicable deadline separately.

To: [original office or appropriate supervisory/grievance officer]
Date: [date]
Subject: Follow-up on complaint [reference] dated [date] concerning [issue/location]

I submitted the attached complaint to [office] on [date]. Its acknowledgement/reference is [number].

Since then:
[List responses, inspections, promised dates and relevant developments chronologically. If a closure response is disputed, explain the specific discrepancy and attach current evidence.]

Current situation:
[What remains unresolved; whether the activity or impact continues.]

Please:
1. Confirm the present status and the office responsible for the next action.
2. Explain what action has been taken and provide or identify the relevant inspection/action record.
3. State the next action and expected timeframe, including any applicable notified service standard.
4. If the case is closed, provide the decision and reasons and identify any applicable review or appeal route.

If addressed to a supervisory office:
I request review of the attached history and appropriate action within your powers. Please let me know whether a prescribed form or separate statutory procedure is required.

Attachments: [original complaint; acknowledgement; replies; indexed new evidence]
Name and contact/address for reply: [details]
Signature: [if on paper]

Keep the delivery evidence and update the case log. A personal follow-up date should not be described as a statutory deadline.
