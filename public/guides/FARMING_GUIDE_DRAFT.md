# Start or sustain farming in Goa

**Draft reviewed against collected sources on 23 October 2026. Legal and service verification remains open.**

Start with the right to use the land, then register the farming activity, get advice for the site and check support before spending. A Krishi Card, a subsidy sanction and a permission to change land use serve different purposes. This guide helps individual growers and community groups organise those steps; it does not certify a land transaction or a scheme entitlement.

## Choose your first step

| Your situation | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| You want to buy, lease or use somebody else's land | Assemble the land records and obtain advice on title, tenancy, transfer restrictions and authority to cultivate. |
| You already cultivate and need registration or support | Approach the Zonal Agriculture Office for the location of the holding, using the [registration enquiry](/guides/templates/09-farming-registration-enquiry.txt). |
| You need crop, soil or pest advice | Contact the relevant Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), Agriculture office or ICAR-CCARI with your crop and site details. |
| A bund has failed, salt water has entered, or crops are damaged | Record the damage promptly and ask the Agriculture office for inspection and the relevant reporting or claim route. |
| You plan a shed, well, pond, filling or tree removal | Check the separate permissions before commissioning work. Use the [building guide](/read/guide-building) and [environmental protection guide](/read/guide-environmental-protection). |

The Directorate's charter lists Zonal Agriculture Offices, soil laboratories, training and subject officers on physical pages 42–43. ICAR-CCARI describes access to crop advice, planting material and publications through its dissemination centre. [Agriculture charter][FR1]; [ICAR services][FR8]

**Source update AG01:** the government reports a newly opened Zonal Agriculture Office at Valpoi on 13 October 2026. Older office lists may need address updates. **Verification needed AG01:** confirm the office serving your holding, exact current address, hours, appointment and submission route. An opening announcement is not a tested service. [Official Valpoi announcement][V12]

## 1. Establish the land and your authority to cultivate

Create a parcel file containing the village, taluka, survey and subdivision numbers, area, current Form I and XIV, survey plan, access details and the documents explaining your interest in the land. Include relevant sale, succession, lease, consent or authority documents. Record any co-owner, tenant, comunidade or other interest requiring examination. Keep conflicting names or areas visible for resolution.

Before signing a transaction, ask a Goa land-law practitioner to check title and existing rights, the validity of the proposed arrangement, and whether permission is required. The Agricultural Tenancy Act is a separate legal framework; an informal consent letter or a registration application does not settle tenancy rights. [Tenancy Act][FR3]

The **2023 agricultural-transfer Act uses a specific definition tied to paddy cultivation**, with an exclusion for land earmarked for other uses in a TCP plan. Its section 3 restricts transfers of land within that definition and provides a Collector-permission mechanism in specified circumstances. Do not apply a blanket “anyone can buy farmland” or “no newcomer can farm” rule. [2023 Act, sections 2–4][FR2]

**Commencement and rules checked:** the Act's Gazette includes notification 16/1/2/2020-Rev-I/Pt.III/489 appointing **18 April 2023** as its commencement date (physical page 4). The **2023 Rules**, published on **19 October 2023**, supply the procedure omitted from the earlier draft. [Act and commencement notice][FR2]; [Rules 3–8 and Forms I–IV][V09]

For a proposed purchase/acquisition, rule 3 lists a Krishi Card, a Mamlatdar certificate, a competent State authority's certificate or another document showing agriculturist status. For the Collector-permission route, rules 5–8 provide Form I, supporting documents and an undertaking, scrutiny, a Mamlatdar-led joint inspection with Agriculture/Talathi participation, and a reasoned permission or refusal. Obtain the actual prescribed form; the guide's enquiry template is not Form I. Rule 4 also requires cultivation to begin within three years of acquisition and continue, with serious consequences for non-compliance; ask the lawyer to explain this duty and the monitoring provisions before committing to a transfer.

**Verification needed AG02:** title, tenancy, the paddy-linked definition and plan exclusion, proposed transaction, buyer status, conditions and later amendments/orders still require a Goa land-law review. A Krishi Card supports the registration question; it is not proof of transferable title or blanket permission to build.

Write down a simple proposed activity: crops, cultivated area, water source, intended structures, earthworks and whether any produce will be processed or sold through a new business. This makes separate permission questions easier to answer.

## 2. Apply for or update the Krishi Card

Ask the Zonal Agriculture Office for the current form and checklist for your applicant type. The collected charter distinguishes an individual farmer from an institution, NGO, self-help group or company. Its checklist includes land records, the applicable document supporting use of the holding, identity and bank information, a mandate and soil samples. Group applications also need registration and authority documents. [Charter, physical page 2][FR1]

Official starting forms are available for an [individual farmer][FR4] and a [farmers group][FR5]. Take these as starting documents and confirm that the receiving office still accepts them.

1. List all holdings accurately, using the same identifiers as the supporting records.
2. Ask how to handle joint, inherited, leased or disputed records before signing a declaration.
3. Ask which originals must be shown and which copies are retained. Share identity and bank documents only through the confirmed official channel.
4. Obtain the prescribed bank mandate and sampling instructions.
5. Submit the accepted application, retain a full copy and obtain a dated receipt or reference.
6. Check the issued card or record for correct names, holdings and bank details; request corrections in writing.

**Published checklist checked:** the charter's individual list includes nominee bank/identity details and soil samples for each survey number. Its group list instead includes the group's registration and authority documents. These were previously left as wholly unresolved; the published distinction is now recorded. **Verification needed AG03:** ask the receiving office which version applies, how to provide samples, which identity alternatives it accepts, and whether fees or further documents apply. Actual processing time and bank-payment mapping remain untested. [Charter, physical page 2][FR1]

A June 2025 Agriculture notice asks farmers to update Aadhaar-seeded bank details through the Zonal office and warns that a later bank-seeding change can change the account receiving benefits. Ask both the bank and the office to confirm the relevant account and mapping. Do not disclose an OTP or banking password to a helper. [Official bank-update notice][FR6]

## 3. Get a soil and crop plan before buying inputs

Ask the laboratory or extension officer how to collect and label samples for each distinct field or management area, what quantity is needed, what tests they offer, and whether water testing is appropriate. Avoid mixing visibly different soils into one sample without their advice. Keep the sample reference linked to the survey number and field sketch.

ICAR's laboratory directory lists testing at KVK North Goa in Ela, the Agriculture Farmers Training Centre in Ela, and KVK South Goa in Margao. Use the directory to choose an enquiry route; verify current reception, charges and turnaround. [Laboratory directory][FR7]

Bring the crop history, irrigation source, flooding or salinity observations, photographs and soil report to the advisory visit. Ask for a locally suitable crop or variety, season, water plan, nutrient recommendation, pest-monitoring method and harvest plan. ICAR-CCARI publishes recommended practices for Goa crops and offers technology advice. [ICAR services][FR8]

Use the [farm planning and activity log](/guides/templates/10-farm-plan-and-log.md). Record why an input was recommended, the product and batch, date, quantity and area treated. Request crop-specific advice before using pesticides; follow the approved label and applicable restrictions. The charter treats seed, fertilizer, insecticide and nursery businesses as separate licensing subjects, so growing crops does not automatically authorise selling regulated inputs. [Charter, physical pages 33–41][FR1]

**Verification needed AG04:** confirm the current crop recommendation, authorised input and use, any harvest interval, and laboratory service details with the appropriate specialist. No pesticide dose, fertilizer rate or yield promise is supplied here. An “organic” marketing or certification claim needs its own applicable standards check.

## 4. Ask about support before committing money

Use the [scheme and damage enquiry](/guides/templates/11-farming-support-or-damage.txt) to request the current notification for the particular activity. The charter describes support across crops, irrigation, machinery, community farming, organic inputs and khazan protection. Treat those headings as questions to ask, not a guarantee that a budget or application window is open. [Agriculture charter][FR1]

For each possible scheme, obtain written answers to:

- Who can apply, and which holding, crop, area or group conditions apply?
- Is prior approval or an inspection needed before purchase or work starts?
- What is the application deadline, eligible cost, contribution, ceiling and funding availability?
- Are particular suppliers, specifications, invoices, payment methods or photographs required?
- Can the same expense receive another subsidy? What must be disclosed?
- What triggers payment, what evidence closes the claim, and how can a rejection be reviewed?

Keep the notification, application, acknowledgement, sanction, invoices and payment record together. A received application is not a sanction. A sanction is not proof of payment. Budget using your confirmed contribution and the actual payment conditions.

**Scheme-existence check:** the Agriculture reply to the Assembly dated 9 March 2026 lists current support categories including Shetkari Adhar Nidhi, horticulture, irrigation and agricultural mechanisation. This is evidence of the programme list, not your eligibility or available funds. **Verification needed AG05:** obtain the relevant current notification, application window, budget availability, prior-sanction conditions, amount and claim process before spending. [2026 Assembly reply][V11]

## 5. Treat water, khazan works and farm structures separately

For a proposed well or groundwater extraction, ask the Water Resources Department which registration or permission applies to the location and intended use. Goa has a Ground Water Regulation Act and Rules; an agriculture scheme sanction does not answer that separate question. [WRD legislation index][FR9]

Before cutting a slope or filling low-lying land, obtain advice from TCP about section 17A and any other applicable controls. Before building a shed or changing use, follow the [building guide](/read/guide-building). For tree removal, coastal land, mangroves, forest or a wetland setting, follow the [environmental guide](/read/guide-environmental-protection). [TCP section 17A procedure][FR10]

For khazan land, identify the bund, sluice, responsible association or authority, and affected cultivators. Ask the Agriculture Soil Conservation Division and relevant revenue authority who may authorise the repair and how costs and responsibilities are shared. The charter has a separate khazan-protection section. Do not breach a bund, change a sluice setting or fill a channel on the assumption that a farm needs it. [Charter, physical pages 30–31 and 42][FR1]

**Verification needed AG06:** verify the actual water/land status, tenants' association or other management rights, current repair scheme and overlapping permissions. Obtain a site-specific answer before physical work.

## 6. Record damage and follow a claim through

If safe, photograph damage before clearing it. Note when it began, the crop and stage, affected area, water level or other observations, and any action already taken. Keep purchase, cultivation and harvest records. Distinguish observed damage from an unconfirmed cause.

Notify the Zonal Agriculture Office promptly and ask for an inspection, the relevant disaster-relief, crop-loss or insurance route, and the actual deadline. For an insured crop, notify the insurer through its policy channel as well; a departmental complaint may not satisfy an insurance condition. For wildlife damage, contact the Forest Department for the applicable route and safe response. Do not poison, trap or injure wildlife.

Use the [reporting guide](/read/guide-reporting) for a numbered follow-up or a targeted RTI request for inspection and decision records. Record an office-promised date separately from a statutory or scheme deadline.

### Shetkari Adhar Nidhi crop-loss route

The revised scheme published on **2 June 2022**, still included in the March 2026 official scheme list, provides a specific starting procedure. It says it continues until withdrawn. The portal's December 2023 upload date is not the notification date. [Scheme, physical pages 1–4][V10]; [2026 programme list][V11]

- Apply to the taluka Zonal Agricultural Officer **as early as possible or within 15 working days of the calamity**, using prescribed Annexure I, estimated losses and representative damage photographs with the applicant or representative.
- The scheme requires a valid Krishi Card and provides for official inspection and assessment. Disclose other relief/insurance because the scheme provides for adjustment.
- Preserve the date of loss, submission receipt, crop/area records, photographs and assessment reference. The supporting template below helps prepare the facts; it does not replace Annexure I.

The older seven-day guidance belongs to an earlier version. **Verification needed AG07:** confirm any later amendment, how working days are counted, the event/crop's eligibility, assessment, funding and interactions with insurance. Do not wait for that enquiry to notify damage promptly. A claim under another scheme or insurance policy may have a different deadline. Compensation is not automatic.

## Worked example

A group wants to restart cultivation on a low-lying holding and buy a pump. First, establish who can authorise cultivation and whether tenancy or transfer restrictions affect the arrangement. Then ask the Zonal office about group registration and the current pump scheme, and obtain soil/water advice. Check any well, electricity, bund or earthwork permissions separately. Order equipment only after the scheme's required approval stage is clear. Keep the receipt, inspection and payment evidence in one file.
