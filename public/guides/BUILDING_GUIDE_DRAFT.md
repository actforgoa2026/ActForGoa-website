# Plan building or repair work in Goa

**Draft for Act for Goa. Source review: 22 October 2026.**

Use this guide before commissioning building work, an extension, reconstruction, a change of use or repairs. It helps you identify the land and responsible authorities, assemble the application, check what must happen before work starts, and obtain the required completion and occupancy documents.

This is the standard planning and local-licensing route. Special procedures and exemptions may apply. **Verification needed** notes identify unresolved legal or service details in the [verification register](/read/guide-verification). A particular plot's title, zoning, permissions and environmental restrictions require case-specific checking by the relevant authority and qualified professionals.

## The usual sequence

1. Identify the plot, proposed work and existing permissions.
2. Establish which planning authority and local body cover the site.
3. Check land use, title/tenure, conversion and environmental restrictions.
4. Obtain the applicable planning permission or technical clearance and other approvals at the required stages.
5. Obtain the local building licence or repair permission and satisfy its conditions before the relevant work.
6. Build to the sanctioned plans, give required stage notices and keep inspection records.
7. Obtain the applicable completion order and local occupancy certificate before occupation.

The planning and local-body stages are distinct in the [TCP procedure][B1]. The detailed requirements must be read with the [building regulations][B2], amendments and the conditions of each approval.

## Identify the work before choosing a form

Write a one-paragraph scope: what exists, what you want to change, the proposed use, approximate area and whether structural work, new floors, subdivision, excavation, filling or tree removal is involved. Photograph the existing condition and collect any approved plans and licences.

| Proposed work | First question to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| New building, reconstruction or extension | Which planning approval and local construction licence apply? |
| Repairs to an existing building | Does the proposed scope qualify as repairs, and what permission is required? |
| Change of use or subdivision | What separate planning, land-use and local permissions apply? |
| Hill cutting, filling or tree removal | What separate permission is required before that activity? |
| An existing building with missing or disputed permissions | What is its documented status, and what lawful route, if any, applies? |

**Verification needed B02:** do not label structural alteration or expansion as “repairs” yourself. Margao publishes a repair procedure involving ownership/survey documents, photographs, an engineer's stability certificate, inspection and permission. Its requirements are a local example, not a statewide checklist. The Panchayat charter also describes a limited repair route, but its current scope and conditions need confirmation. [Margao repair procedure][B3]; [Panchayat charter, printed pages 24–25][B4]

## Establish the two responsible authorities

Ask which planning area contains the site. A village address does not by itself establish TCP jurisdiction, and a municipal address does not by itself establish PDA jurisdiction.

| Planning jurisdiction | Standard planning stage | Local stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An area under a Planning and Development Authority (PDA) | Apply to that PDA for development permission. | Apply to the relevant Panchayat, municipal council or Corporation for the local licence/permit. |
| An area under TCP rather than a PDA | Apply to the responsible Town and Country Planning office for technical clearance. | Apply to the relevant Panchayat, municipal council or Corporation for the local licence/permit. |

This distinction comes from the [official TCP procedure][B1]. Ask for confirmation using the village/town, survey/subdivision number and location plan. Use the [project and permission enquiry template](/guides/templates/06-project-enquiry.txt) to record the answer.

**Verification needed B01:** confirm the current jurisdiction and plan applicable to the particular property. **B02:** the TCP downloads page also lists Notary Architect Engineer forms for certain single-family dwellings. The eligibility, current operation and conditions of that special route have not been verified; this guide does not assume it is available. [TCP forms][B5]

## Check the land before committing to the design

Build a property file with your registered professional and, for title or tenure questions, a lawyer experienced in Goa land matters.

| Check | Obtain or establish | Keep in the file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact parcel and access | Survey/subdivision or city-survey identifiers, survey plan, boundaries and documented access | Certified/current records and the plan used for the application |
| Right to carry out the work | Ownership or other legally sufficient interest; relevant succession, co-owner or authority documents | The instrument and advice establishing the applicant's authority |
| Applicable planning controls | Current zoning information, relevant Regional/Outline Development Plan, amendments and any site-specific orders | Official extracts and the authority's written response |
| Land conversion | Whether conversion is required, already granted, or subject to restrictions | Applicable conversion sanad and conditions, or written clarification of the route |
| Other restrictions | Agricultural tenure/transfer, comunidade or mundkar issues, forest status, coastal or wetland restrictions, heritage constraints and any court orders that affect the property | Relevant records, approvals and unresolved questions |

TCP's published document list includes survey and record-of-rights material, evidence of interest in the land, professional certificates and conversion documentation. These papers serve different purposes: ask the professionals to establish both the right to develop and the permitted development. [Technical-clearance procedure PDF][B6]; [Land Revenue Code and Rules][B7]

For agricultural land, obtain specific advice on the [2023 transfer restrictions][B8] and other applicable tenancy/land-use rules. Owning a property, holding a tax receipt or seeing a settlement colour on a map does not by itself demonstrate that the proposed building is permitted. Ask which documents establish each required element rather than treating one document as approval for everything.

**Verification needed B03:** the applicable zoning, conversion, tenure, environmental boundaries and any litigation are plot-specific. Do not use a draft map or draft wetland notification as evidence of final legal status. The corpus is a reference library, not a parcel-level clearance service.

## Screen for additional permissions

Tell the designer and authorities about these features at the start:

- **Coastal location:** establish the applicable coastal plan/classification and whether GCZMA approval is required. Use the official maps and site records, not a guessed distance from the water.
- **Trees or forest land:** check tree-felling/removal permission and any separate forest restriction. A building licence should not be assumed to authorise these activities.
- **Hill cutting or filling low-lying land:** TCP publishes a separate section 17A permission process and supporting-plan requirements. Raise this even if the work is described as levelling the plot. [Section 17A procedure][B9]
- **Wetlands, drains and watercourses:** establish the applicable protection and drainage requirements before changing ground levels or drainage.
- **Heritage/conservation, fire and other restrictions:** ask whether conservation review, fire clearance, airport/defence/railway permissions or other NOCs apply. [Building regulations][B2]

**Verification needed B03 and B04:** the applicability and sequence of these approvals depend on the site and project. An unresolved restriction is a question to settle before the affected work, not an item to defer until occupancy. No blanket setback, floor-area ratio, height, CRZ distance or exemption is stated here.

## Assemble and submit the application

Engage professionals registered and competent for the relevant work. Ask who will prepare the plans, take structural responsibility, supervise construction and certify completion. Registration and competence categories appear in the [TCP downloads][B5].

Use the [application and pre-work checklist](/guides/templates/07-building-checklist.md) to organise:

- The current application and questionnaire for the relevant authority.
- Land/title/authority documents and current survey/record material.
- Site, floor, elevation and section drawings, drainage/services details and structural material required for the proposal.
- Owner declarations and the applicable professional certificates.
- Conversion documents and relevant NOCs/approvals.
- The fee assessment and receipts.

This is a preparation list. The authority's current checklist determines the required set, format, certification and number of copies. [Technical-clearance procedure PDF][B6]

### Official forms to start from

| Stage | Published form or entry |
| --- | --- |
| PDA development permission | [Application under section 44][F1] |
| TCP technical clearance | Find the applicable TCP application and current procedure in [official downloads][B5]; confirm whether the current electronic route replaces the paper form. |
| Local village licence | [Village Panchayat building licence application][F2] |
| Municipal licence | [Municipal Council building licence application][F3]; a Corporation may require its own route. |
| Conversion recommendation | [Recommendation form][F4]; distinguish a planning recommendation from the conversion sanad issued under the revenue process. |
| Occupancy application | [Municipal Council/Village Panchayat application][F5] |

**Verification needed B05:** forms linked by an official site may still be older versions. Confirm the accepted form, current online/paper process, enclosures and copies with the receiving authority. TCP links to BPAMS; its submission process and current coverage were not tested. Use the TCP website to reach it rather than an unofficial intermediary. [TCP downloads][B5]

At each submission, retain the complete version submitted, acknowledgement, application number and payment receipt. If more information is requested, answer by reference to the same file and save the revised version. Ask which stage has been approved and which approvals remain outstanding.

## Check the approval before work starts

Read the permission and licence with the sanctioned plans. Use the pre-work checklist to confirm:

1. The applicant, survey number, site, approved use and plan version match your project.
2. The necessary planning permission/technical clearance and local licence are in place for the work being started.
3. Conditions that apply before that work, including conversion and conditional environmental/NOC requirements, have been satisfied.
4. The work falls within the approval's validity and scope; any renewal or amendment is dealt with.
5. The professional responsible for supervision knows the inspection and stage-notice requirements.

**Verification needed B04:** recent building-regulation amendments are in the corpus, including a TCP-hosted notification dated 6 August 2026. Their commencement, effect and any related court directions have not been fully consolidated. The designer must use the rules legally applicable to the project, not just the original 2010 document. [TCP regulations and amendments][B10]

**Verification needed B06:** fees, time limits, renewals and appeal rights vary by service and approval. Ask for the applicable notification and a written fee calculation. The Panchayat Act contains specific appeal/deeming provisions concerning permission applications; a receipt or elapsed time alone is not enough to establish that those conditions have been met. Get case-specific advice before relying on a deemed approval. [Panchayat Act, section 66][B11]

Regularisation of an existing unauthorised structure is a separate question. Do not treat the existence of a regularisation law or payment demand as advance permission for new work.

## Keep records during construction

Keep the sanctioned plans and approvals available to the supervising professional. Record stage notices, inspections, directions, revised approvals and dated site photographs. TCP publishes a stage-intimation form, while the Panchayat charter describes commencement, plinth and completion notices. Confirm the stages and submission method specified for your project. [TCP downloads][B5]; [Panchayat charter, printed page 24][B4]

Ask for the required approval before departing from the approved design or use. Record the authority's answer; do not assume a change can be settled at completion. Keep excavation, drainage, tree and waste conditions with the main site documents so the contractor can follow them.

**Verification needed B07:** confirm applicable construction-worker registration, cess, safety and waste obligations for the project. This draft does not calculate them. The corpus includes Goa construction-worker rules and newer construction-and-demolition waste rules; their application needs a separate project check. [Labour Department charter][B12]; [construction waste rules][B13]

## Complete the approval process before occupation

Plan the handover early. Under the ordinary process, the planning authority's completion order and the local body's occupancy certificate are distinct documents. A contractor's handover or a professional's completion certificate is not a substitute for both authority stages where they are required. [TCP procedure][B1]

Ask the supervising professional for the relevant completion certification, structural-stability certification and final/as-built drawings, including any approved revisions. Obtain the applicable planning completion order, then apply to the local body for occupancy and satisfy inspection and clearance requirements. Use the [completion and occupancy enquiry](/guides/templates/08-completion-enquiry.txt) to establish the accepted checklist. [Technical-clearance procedure PDF][B6]; [occupancy application][F5]

Keep the issued orders, approved final plans, certificates, conditions and receipts in the property file. Verify whether occupancy is full or partial and whether any conditions remain.

**Verification needed B08:** confirm the current completion/occupancy route for the authority, project category and any special approval scheme. Separate operating licences may be needed for business use; occupancy is not a universal licence to run a commercial activity.

## If you are checking somebody else's construction

Use the [reporting guide](/read/guide-reporting). Identify the parcel, record what is visible from a lawful location, request permissions and plans from the authorities, and describe suspected discrepancies factually. The absence of a visible notice board alone does not prove there is no permission. Ask the authority to inspect rather than making a definitive accusation yourself.
