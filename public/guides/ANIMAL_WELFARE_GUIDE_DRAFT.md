# Help an animal and report cruelty in Goa

**Draft reviewed against collected sources on 22 October 2026. Contact availability and current local implementation still need verification.**

Choose the response by the situation: a person exposed to rabies needs medical care; an injured domestic animal needs veterinary assistance; cruelty may need police action; wildlife needs the Forest Department. An organisation's listing does not establish that it can dispatch a vehicle or accept an animal.

## Start with the immediate need

| Situation | First action |
| --- | --- |
| A person has been bitten, scratched or exposed to potentially infectious saliva | Wash the exposed wound and seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for animal rescue or observation. |
| A domestic animal is injured, sick or trapped | Contact the nearest appropriate veterinary service; describe the location, species, condition and access. Confirm who can attend or receive it. |
| You witness violence, suspected poisoning or serious neglect | Request police assistance and veterinary assessment; document facts safely. |
| A snake, wild bird or other wildlife is injured or inside a building | Keep people and pets away and contact Forest Department rescue services. Avoid handling it. |
| Cattle or dogs create an immediate road hazard | Report the precise road and direction to police/road responders and the local body; request humane animal handling. Do not enter traffic to capture them. |
| You need sterilisation, vaccination or help with a community-dog dispute | Contact the local body and jurisdictional veterinary officer; ask which recognised programme and current directions apply. |

The AHVS [cruelty and distress contact page][AN1] links to veterinary, hospital, Panchayat, police, DSPCA and nodal-officer routes. Its [veterinary directory][AN2] lists facilities by location. Use these to choose a relevant service, then confirm availability.

## Human exposure needs medical care now

After a bite or scratch, wash the wound thoroughly with soap and running water for **at least 15 minutes** and seek urgent medical assessment for rabies post-exposure care. Saliva on broken skin or a mucous membrane can also be an exposure. A clinician determines the vaccine and any immunoglobulin or monoclonal-antibody treatment needed; do not wait for symptoms or for a complaint to be registered. [WHO rabies guidance, prevention and post-exposure prophylaxis][AN3]

Tell the clinician when and how the exposure happened and any previous rabies vaccination. Keep the treatment record and follow the schedule provided. Report the animal's location separately to the veterinary/local authority without attempting to capture it yourself. For immediate danger to people, use **112**; Goa also lists **108/102** for ambulance services. [Goa emergency numbers][AN4]

**Verification needed AW01:** a Goa clinician should check the final medical wording and current treatment-centre referral information. No facility's vaccine stock, immunoglobulin availability or opening hours has been confirmed. The emergency numbers are published channels, not tested dispatches.

## Find the right animal service

### Domestic animals

Use the [AHVS emergency-contact directory][AN2] to locate the veterinary hospital or dispensary serving the area. The state portal also lists office contacts for Tonca, Mapusa, Sonsodo and Curti-Ponda. AHVS head office publishes **9607948546 / 8956863414** and **dir-ahvs.goa@nic.in** for departmental enquiries; these are not established here as round-the-clock rescue numbers. [Department directory][AN5]; [AHVS head office][AN6]

Before moving an animal, ask:

- Can you attend this location, or which facility can receive this species now?
- Is a trained handler or suitable vehicle needed, and who will arrange it?
- Are there charges, admission conditions or an owner-consent requirement?
- Who is the responsible contact, and what is the case or referral number?

Use the [animal assistance request and handover record](/guides/templates/12-animal-assistance.txt). Do not give medicines, induce vomiting or attempt a hazardous capture based on this guide. If poisoning is suspected, keep people and other animals away from the suspected material and ask the veterinarian/police how evidence should be preserved.

### Wildlife

The Forest Department publishes **0832-2228772 for North Goa** and **0832-2750246 for South Goa**, labelled as 24-hour helplines. Its general office contact is **0832-2492533**. Give the village, map pin, species if known and immediate risk. If you cannot identify the animal, describe it and send a photograph only if safe. [Forest contact page][AN7]

Keep your distance; avoid crowding, feeding, capturing or relocating the animal. Ask the department to arrange or identify an authorised responder. A volunteer contact or social-media post is not a wildlife possession or relocation permission. The official Forest site provides the wildlife-law and protected-area references. [Forest Department][AN8]

**Verification needed AW02:** the source-listed numbers, directory assignments, service boundaries, response times, transport, fees and responder authorisation need direct verification. If one route fails, try the relevant local veterinary office or Forest office and record the referral. Escalate immediate human danger through emergency services.

## Report suspected cruelty with usable evidence

The Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act places a duty on people in charge of animals to take reasonable measures for their wellbeing and to prevent unnecessary pain or suffering. Section 11 addresses specified forms of cruel treatment. Use observed facts, not a guessed legal charge. [PCA Act, sections 3 and 11][AN9]

1. Record the time, exact location, species and number of animals, and what you personally observed. Mark second-hand reports as such.
2. Preserve original photographs or video taken from a lawful, safe position. Record injuries as observations; a veterinarian should diagnose and document them.
3. Where possible, record a vehicle, premises or person identifier accurately. Do not guess identities or publish private details in an appeal for help.
4. Contact the jurisdictional police station for suspected offences, and AHVS for veterinary assistance and the appropriate welfare authority. The official cruelty page includes police, DSPCA and nodal contacts. Wildlife cases should also go to Forest authorities. [AHVS routes][AN1]; [Goa cruelty SOP][AN10]
5. Submit the [cruelty complaint](/guides/templates/13-animal-cruelty-complaint.txt), request acknowledgement and ask what complaint/FIR or other record has been made, as applicable.
6. Ask for veterinary examination and preservation of relevant evidence through the authorities. Do not seize an animal or enter private premises yourself.

The **2020 Goa SOP contains historical criminal-law references**. It supports coordination, but its old offence numbers should not be copied into a current complaint. Police or a lawyer should identify the current provisions and procedure. [Goa SOP][AN10]

**Verification needed AW03:** confirm current criminal-law provisions, cognizability and police procedure for the actual facts, competent local welfare bodies and the latest SOP. This guide does not promise that every allegation must result in the same criminal process, or that a complainant may take custody.

If no action follows, send a dated factual follow-up with the original reference and new evidence. Use the [reporting guide](/read/guide-reporting) and [case log](/guides/templates/05-case-log.md). Obtain legal advice promptly about urgent protection, an adverse decision or a refused criminal complaint; the [Goa legal-aid service][AN11] explains its assistance and eligibility.

## Request community-animal care through the local programme

Ask the Panchayat, municipality or other responsible local authority for the jurisdictional veterinary officer, the programme operator, current project recognition and complaint route. The ABC Rules 2023 provide for project recognition, local-authority responsibilities, veterinary care, records and monitoring. General NGO recognition and recognition for an ABC project are different questions. [ABC Rules, rules 3, 8–14 and schedules][AN12]

Use the [community-animal service enquiry](/guides/templates/14-community-animal-service.txt) to request:

- A vaccination or sterilisation assessment, describing identifiable animals and known medical needs.
- The responsible centre and veterinary supervisor, with the recognised project details.
- Capture, transport, treatment, recovery and individual-record arrangements.
- The lawful destination after treatment, identifying the site category and applicable directions.
- A contact for missed service, injury, unsafe handling or disagreement.

Do not organise an informal capture or move dogs to another locality. If an authorised operation appears harmful, record the specific conduct safely and use the complaint route. A disputed removal decision and alleged cruelty during handling need to be described separately.

## Check current directions before advising on feeding or release

The **Supreme Court judgment of 19 May 2026, 2026 INSC 506**, upholds the institutional-premises directions considered there, including the prohibition on re-release at those locations. It also directs authorities to assess extension to other high-footfall public spaces and places ongoing compliance oversight with High Courts. It does not justify a resident deciding a relocation rule on their own. [Judgment, paragraphs 75–76, 108(D) and 111][AN13]

Consequently, do not publish a universal “release every dog at the same spot” rule or a universal feeding entitlement from an older leaflet. For a school, hospital, transport facility or other disputed place, ask the responsible authority for the applicable order, site designation, nodal officer and humane management arrangements. For a residential feeding dispute, ask for the applicable designated arrangement and dispute process, taking account of the current court directions as well as the rules. [ABC Rules, rule 20][AN12]; [2026 judgment][AN13]

**Verification needed AW04:** retrieve and reconcile the latest Supreme Court, Bombay High Court/Goa orders, AWBI institutional SOP and local implementation decisions before giving site-specific advice. The May judgment is verified source material, not a certification that no later order exists. No operational euthanasia advice is provided; such decisions require the competent veterinary and legal process.

## Keep care and custody records

For a pet or livestock animal in your charge, agree a vaccination, preventive-care and identification plan with a veterinarian. Keep proof of ownership or lawful handover, medical records and emergency contacts. Check local registration requirements, travel rules or business permissions before breeding, sale, boarding or transport. The duty of care applies while an animal is in your custody. [PCA Act, section 3][AN9]

For a rescue handover, record who accepted the animal, where, when, its identifiers and condition, and the agreed next update. Distinguish temporary care from an ownership transfer. Do not promise shelter admission, adoption approval, reimbursement or a right to reclaim without the receiving authority's process.

**Verification needed AW05:** confirm species-specific transport, adoption, livestock, pet-registration and commercial-activity requirements for the actual case. These subjects need separate checklists if Act for Goa expands this guide.

## Worked example and templates

For an injured dog near a school, arrange urgent veterinary help and tell the school/local-body nodal contact where it is. Record any human exposure for medical care. Ask the authority for the applicable institutional process and treatment destination; do not assume the dog will be returned to the school. Record the handover and request a case update. Report any observed cruelty separately with evidence.
