## Priority work for local reviewers

1. **Land and building decisions:** a Goa land/planning lawyer and registered designer should confirm the full amendment chain, tenure, plan boundaries and permissions for a real plot. Start with B01–B04, B06, AG02 and AG06.
2. **Courts and legal clocks:** obtain current advice on RTI disclosure/appeals, institutional animal directions and any environmental litigation before relying on a remedy or deadline. Start with R04–R05, AW03–AW04 and EP08.
3. **Medical and rescue routes:** a Goa clinician should review the exposure wording; contact authorised facilities to confirm treatment, hours, stocks, rescue transport and admission. Start with AW01–AW02.
4. **Office and scheme acceptance:** confirm current forms, payments, counters, Krishi/bank mapping, subsidy windows and crop-loss reporting with the receiving offices. Start with R01–R03, B05–B08 and AG01–AG07.
5. **Site protections and local services:** obtain the actual map/notification/court package and responsible officer for the site, then confirm waste collection, tree processes, participation deadlines and biodiversity access. Start with EP01–EP07.

For each direct check, save the date, office/designation, exact question, written reply or receipt reference, and who reviewed it. Record “no response” separately from “confirmed”. Do not publish personal staff details without a reason and permission.
