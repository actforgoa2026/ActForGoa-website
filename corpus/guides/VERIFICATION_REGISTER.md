# Verification register for the practical guides

**Review date: 22 October 2026. Status: drafts for editorial and legal review.**

“Source checked” means the indicated published material was inspected. It does not mean an office answered a call, accepted an application or confirmed a particular property's legal position. No complaints, applications or messages were sent to authorities.
