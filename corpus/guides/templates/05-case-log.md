# Complaint and application log

Keep a separate row for each submission or response. Record complaint, RTI and appeal deadlines separately. Dates you choose as reminders are not statutory deadlines.

| Date | Case type and reference | Authority and submission method | Document or action | Receipt or evidence location | Official deadline or promised date and its source | Personal reminder | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [date] | [complaint / RTI / appeal / permission] | [office; email / portal / inward / post] | [what happened] | [saved file or receipt] | [date; rule, order or officer's response; or not confirmed] | [date] | [action] |

For RTI, record the authority's receipt date, any transfer, fee notice and payment, the response, and the appeal decision date. For a building file, record the exact approved plan version and any conditions with a deadline.
