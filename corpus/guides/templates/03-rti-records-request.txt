DRAFT TEMPLATE: RTI REQUEST FOR EXISTING RECORDS
For an Indian citizen. Address it to the relevant public authority's SPIO. Select the relevant questions; do not send every example indiscriminately. Follow the accepted form and fee/payment requirements.

To: The State Public Information Officer
[Public authority and address]
Date: [date]
Subject: Application under section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005

I am an Indian citizen and request the following records held by your authority.

Property/matter: [village/town, survey/subdivision number and location, or complaint reference]
Period covered: [start date to end date]

Select and adapt:
1. Copies of the development permission/technical clearance, building licence, sanctioned plans and approved amendments relating to [identified property/application].
2. Copies of inspection reports and recorded findings concerning [identified property or complaint].
3. Copies of notices, replies, orders, recorded decisions and action-taken records relating to complaint [reference/date].
4. A copy of the rule, circular, notified service standard or published checklist used by your authority for [identified service or decision].
5. The record identifying the office/designation to which complaint [reference] was assigned and the dates of recorded transfers or action.

Preferred access: [electronic copies where held and available in that form / certified paper copies / inspection followed by selected copies].

If any requested part is held by another public authority and transfer is applicable under section 6(3), please transfer that part and inform me. If disclosure of a part is refused, please identify the applicable provision, give the decision and reasons, and supply any reasonably separable disclosable portion under section 10. Where relevant, please consider section 8(2); any specific public-interest grounds are: [state factual grounds or remove]. Please provide the relevant appeal particulars.

Please inform me of any additional fee with its calculation and payment instructions before supplying chargeable copies.

Application fee: [accepted payment mode and receipt/reference]
OR fee-exemption claim: [BPL status and applicable proof; remove if not applicable]

Name: [name]
Postal address for correspondence: [address]
Email: [if desired]
Phone: [if desired/required by the selected portal]
Signature: [if on paper]

Attachments: [fee receipt/exemption proof; minimal identifying location/reference material]

Keep separately: full submitted application, receipt, date of actual receipt by the authority, registration number, transfer/additional-fee notices and calculated response deadline.

This template requests records. Submit a separate complaint if you also want inspection or corrective action. Access remains subject to the Act's disclosure, exemption and third-party provisions.

The personal-information exemption was amended through DPDP Act section 44(3). Avoid unnecessary personal identifiers; request redacted records where appropriate. Disclosure and public-interest balancing require a decision under the current law.
