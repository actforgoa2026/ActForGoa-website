# Act for Goa draft practical guides

Prepared on 22 October 2026; source verification updated on 23 October 2026 from the research corpus, existing Act for Goa resources and official published sources. These remain reviewable drafts. Their website versions retain the verification flags and do not constitute consolidated legal advice.

- [Report a civic or environmental problem](REPORTING_GUIDE_DRAFT.md): routing, evidence, submission, follow-up, RTI and appeals.
- [Plan building or repair work](BUILDING_GUIDE_DRAFT.md): land and authority checks, permissions, forms, construction records, completion and occupancy.
- [Start or sustain farming](FARMING_GUIDE_DRAFT.md): land rights, registration, advice, support, water and damage records.
- [Help animals and report cruelty](ANIMAL_WELFARE_GUIDE_DRAFT.md): medical/veterinary/wildlife routes, cruelty evidence, community animals and care records.
- [Protect the environment](ENVIRONMENTAL_PROTECTION_GUIDE_DRAFT.md): site protections, permissions, complaints, public participation and biodiversity.
- [23 October source review](SOURCES_VERIFIED.md): newly resolved questions, official references and evidence limits.
- [Verification register](VERIFICATION_REGISTER.md): unresolved questions, supporting evidence and suggested reviewers.
- [Source register](SOURCES.md): official links, local originals and the sections used.
- [Additional source register](SOURCES_NEXT.md): farming, animal welfare and environmental protection sources.
