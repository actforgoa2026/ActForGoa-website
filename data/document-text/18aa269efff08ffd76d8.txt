

  
2022-2023  is the year ActfoGoa finally decided to give our platform and
organization some much-needed structural support. We have been formally
adopted by Kokum Trust as its official institutionalized parent organization. It
was a no-brainer really as two of the ActForGoa Stewards are the founding and
active sitting Board Members of Kokum Trust. We are beyond excited to
officially announce this formal tie-up, as it means we are now officially in a
position to raise funds and help our programs increase their impact. Through
our efforts over the last year, Kokum Trust has gotten its 12A, 80G, and CSR
certificate making us fully compliant and capable to receive any and all
donations.
In addition, to creating a solid institutionalized foundation for ActforGoa's
ongoing operations, we also began to formally work with other NGOs,
government bodies, and non-profits. We launched some of the first
collaborated events last April when we hosted an Eco Film Festival with the
Jnana Foundation in honor of  Earth Day. Then later in honor of World
Environment Day, we partnered with an additional six organizations to organize
five massive clean-ups across the state. Later we took this theme of Waste
Management even further by formally tieing up with the Forca Goa Foundation
to help them run and expand their Goa vs. Garbage campaign. To bring more
legitimacy and make sure we were helping to reinforce the messaging of the
Goa Government we reached out to the Goa Waste Management Corporation,
GIZ, and Circular Waste Solutions who helped us develop more impactful
infographics on waste management in Goa. We created a workshop series and
workbook for the kids involved in the Forca Goa Foundation's Little Guars
League, where we discovered the girls in particular were incredibly perceptive
and ready to become waste management champions. 
Now that we have institutionalized ourselves, we are beyond excited about
what possibilities lay in the year to come. We want to continue to go deeper
with our work and build even stronger partnerships with other organizations.
We believe we can use our unique placement in Goa and skillset as effective
collaborators to act as an impact multiplier. We believe ActforGoa can become
that bridge between all the various government bodies, non-profits, social
businesses, and concerned citizens.  We are ready to move from a place of
reaction to a proactive one. We want to codesign and cocreate the beautiful
sustainable and just future for Goa, that our amazing home truly deserves.

On the 21st of April 2022, Act for Goa along with Jnana
Foundation hosted an Eco Film Fest at Prana, Anjuna featuring
various films made by local artists and active citizens on issues
affecting Goa’s green ecosystem.
We saw a successful turnout where the audience engaged in
some interesting conversations with those involved in the making
of the films, giving everyone a deeper insight of environmental
issues around Goa and letting us know that it’s high time we
citizens take action for Goa’s sustainable development. As for the
individuals, collectives and organisations involved in the making
of the films, they ranged from students and active youth, to
eminent artists and experts.

On the 23rd of November 2022, Act for Goa, Prana – Anjuna, Terra.Do
and Climate Interactive organised Session 2 of the Goa Climate
Series where the audience discussed key components of the draft
Goa State Action Plan on Climate Change from 2020 – 2030, and
modelled it’s impact on mitigating climate change using the En-
Roads simulator developed by Massachusetts Institute of Technology
(MIT), USA.
 
The session was attended by 15 people from diverse backgrounds
such as tourism, blended finance, Goan based environmental non-
profits, photographers, artists, chefs and so on.
Act for Goa presented some alarming excerpts from the Goa State
Climate Action Plan, followed by usage of the simulator - from this
we learnt that the Goa State Climate Action plan can mitigate
temperature rise from 3.6°C at the end of the century to 2.8°C. Goa,
however, will still face the worst impacts of climate change at a
2.8°C future and the audience concluded that further action needs
to be taken to keep fossil fuels in the ground and to transition the
Goan economy to a low carbon economy dominated by renewable
energy.

On the occasion on World Environment Day, June 5, 2022, Act for
Goa in collaboration with ‘Make my Trip’ Foundation and Circular
Waste Solutions India, hosted a series of clean up drives at five
various locations in North Goa, namely:
1. Chapora Fort
2. Guirim Panchayat Area
3. Mandrem Beach
4. Miramar Beach
5. Bambolim Beach
The drives gained a turnout of 250 odd volunteers where all-in-all,
around 260 bags of garbage were collected, where the volunteers
who showed up ranged from active citizens and students, to
professionals, panchayat members and organisation members &
representatives. The hands at Mandrem beach alone, thanks to the
support of KCIC collected a total of 156 garbage bags.
This initiative was further supported by local and national outfits
such as TERI (The Energy Resources Institute), CCP (Corporation of
the City of Panjim), NAMA Facility, GWMC (Goa Waste Management
Corporation) and GIZ (The Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale
Zusammenarbeit).

In this year of 2022-23, to spread our reach beyond social media,
we at Act for Goa began creating and circulating a newsletter on a
quarterly basis to 800 odd recipients across the globe on recent
activities and doings over at the organisation - many of which
involved call-to-actions. 
Beyond including mere updates, newsletters were created based
on relevant affairs happening in the state of Goa that required
public attention and action. 
For instance, in view of the Panchayat elections in August 2022,
one of our first newsletters was made to be a voters guide aimed at
spreading overall voter awareness & engagement, and to help
Goans be active citizens in democracy. 
It consisted of helpful how-to's and everything one would need to
know as a voter – from documents to be carried when going to
place your vote and voting schedules, to breakdowns of the
Panchayat System and Gram Sabha.
Our newsletters were overall well received and garnered interest
too in the form of responses.

During the month of June 2022, Act for Goa took on an intern -
Saniya from Bombay, pursuing her business course who was
required to be a part of a social volunteer programme as part of
her curriculum. 
Based on her field of interest, we connected Saniya to Dr.
Sabina Martins from the womens movement "Bailancho Saad"
where she shadowed and interviewed Sabina on cases related
to womens safety and well-being in Goa - from domestic abuse
relief to laws and rights of women in the state. This researched
information later fed back into our campaign on 'Women
Empowerment'.
Liesje, a student of development sociology and cultural
anthropology at Radboud University in Nijmegen volunteered
with Act for Goa in the month of March 2023 while working on
her research on nature conservation in Goa - in line with which
she interviewed eminent experts such as Dean D'Cruz
sustainable architect and conservationist, Omkar Dharwadkar a
conservationist and Wildlife Photographer, Dr. Maryanne Lobo,
an expert in agroforestry - all who Act for Goa connected her to.
The transcription of this feeds back into her thesis and doubles
as content that will eventually be used for our organisation's
resources while also educating the public and highlighting such
local experts and their knowledge.

On the 28th of November 2022, Act for Goa was felicitated at the
CleanAThon event among other brilliant NGO’s, organisations and
Panchayats for participating and doing exemplary work for Goa’s
environment. 
CleanAThon is a movement towards cleanliness of our beaches, to
create awareness towards marine life , waste management,
tourism, sustainability, and other areas that need our attention.
This particular CleanAThon event was oraganised by the
Corporation of the City of Panjim (CCP), My Gov Goa, Divyaj
Foundation and Bhamla Foundation, and was the concluding event
of the International Film Festival Goa (IFFI) that was flagged off
Hon. Union Minister Anurag Thakur and & Chief Minister of Goa Dr.
Prahmod Sawant.
Others who joined the were Jackie Shroff, Remo D’Souza, Karan
Kundrra and other dignitaries.
We at Act for Goa were honoured and hope to continue to inspire
and help the people of Goa contribute to a better Goa in every
which way, and hope to see more of such efforts be recognised!
