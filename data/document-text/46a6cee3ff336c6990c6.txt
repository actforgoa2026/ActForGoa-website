The Panchayati Raj system is all about giving
power to the people in our villages. It makes sure
that everyone can help make decisions about how
our village is run. 
Village groups, like Gram Panchayats and Gram
Sabhas, are in charge of making important
choices for the community. This means villagers
can say what they want when it comes to things
like building roads, starting new programs to help
everyone, and collecting money to keep the village
running well.
By letting everyone have a say, this system makes
sure everything is clear and fair. It helps the village
leaders listen to what people need and want.
Villagers can join in and make their home a better
place. Everyone gets to play a part in making the
village grow and improve!

Gram Panchayats Can Collect Money - taxes, fees, &
other charges from the people in the village.
The money collected is important because it helps
pay for things the village needs, like fixing roads,
keeping the village clean, and making sure everyone
has clean water.
When villages collect their own money, they don’t
have to wait for help from the state or central
government. They can use the money right away to
fix problems and make improvements.
Gram Panchayats have financial freedom to  take
care of things like garbage collection, water supply,
and building repairs without delays.
Keeping the Village Happy: By managing their own
funds, Gram Panchayats ensure that the village stays
clean, safe, and happy.

Every five years, villagers get to vote for who will be in
the Gram Panchayat. This voting process is important
because it helps us choose the leaders who will make
decisions for our community. To make sure our village
leadership is fair and includes everyone, some seats in
the Gram Panchayat are reserved for women and
people from groups like Scheduled Castes (SC),
Scheduled Tribes (ST), and Other Backward Classes
(OBC).
Having these special seats is important because it
means everyone gets a chance to be heard, especially
those who might not usually have a say. This system
helps bring in different ideas and perspectives, making
our village leadership more diverse and inclusive. When
people from various backgrounds are part of the
decision-making process, it helps us understand and
address the needs of all community members.
In the end, this approach makes our village a fairer place,
where everyone’s voice matters and everyone’s needs
are considered. This way, we can all work together to
make our community a better place for everyone.

In Goa, several important groups help our villages run
smoothly and fairly. Let's meet these helpers!
First, there's the State Finance Commission. They
make sure our villages get their fair share of money. This
helps us pay for things we need, like clean water, good
roads, and schools.
Next, we have the State Election Commission. They
make sure our village elections are fair and honest.
Thanks to them, we can choose our leaders without any
problems.
Then, there are the District Planning Committees.
They help plan big projects for the entire district. They
take ideas from all the villages and put them together to
make the best plans.
These helpers work together to make sure everything
runs smoothly in our villages.

Important Helpers for Our Village
Gram Sabhas are like big, important meetings for
everyone in the village. If you're an adult and you live in
the village, you're part of the Gram Sabha!
In these meetings, all the adults come together to talk
about what the village needs. They discuss things like
building new roads, fixing schools, starting helpful
projects, and deciding how to spend the village’s money.
For anything big to happen, the Gram Sabha has to
say "yes." This means everyone in the meeting has a
chance to share their ideas and vote on what they think
is best. This way, everyone gets to help decide on
important issues, making sure the village grows in a way
that everyone likes.
So, the Gram Sabha is a way for everyone to work
together to make the village a better place, making
sure all decisions are fair and everyone’s voice is heard!

The Role of Village Development
Committees
The Village Development Committee (VDC) is like a
team of village helpers. Each year, they make a special
plan called the Gram Panchayat Development Plan
(GPDP). This plan lists all the important things we want
to do to make our village better.
The village leader, or Sarpanch, is
the boss of the VDC. They have
meetings every month to check
how things are going and fix any
problems.
The VDC talks to everyone in
the village to make sure the
plan includes what people
need and want.
So, the VDC is a team that plans and works hard to
keep our village a great place to live!
