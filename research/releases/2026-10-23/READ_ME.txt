evidence specified in the notes; it is not necessarily the person's latest work.

Blank means no reliable activity date was established. The research date is separate.

HOW TO USE THE CSV FILES
All files use UTF-8 with a BOM, a header row, quoted CSV fields and CRLF newlines.
Import all columns as TEXT, especially public_phone and record_id. Phone numbers
start with +91; spreadsheet auto-detection can change their display or type.
Within a cell, multiple values are separated by " | ". Empty means no usable value
was established; it does not mean the subject has no website, email or photograph.

record_id is the import key. related_record_ids links a person to an organization,
project or shared office. The opportunities file links back to community records.
Exact normalized name/organization/role duplicates were merged. Similar spellings
are not automatically assumed to be the same person. Preserve record IDs and human
corrections when this becomes a CRM; review identity merges separately.

FIELD GUIDE
record_type: individual, organization, business, network, project, programme,
resource, wildlife_rescue_contact, government office/contact or participation route.
name / organization / role: subject and publicly documented association.
focus_areas: pipe-separated topic tags; environmental_lead and
community_support_or_unclassified_lead are discovery classifications.
goa_location / jurisdiction: locality or area served; no private home addresses.
summary: brief description of the documented work or public function.
public_phone / public_email: published contact routes; inspect contact_kind to
distinguish a direct public contact from a shared office or referral route.
website_url: subject, programme or official directory page where appropriate.
instagram_url / facebook_url / linkedin_url / x_url / youtube_url: attributed public
account links. Publisher share buttons and unrelated accounts were excluded.
profile_photo_url: linked portrait, organization logo or labelled vendor image; not a downloaded asset.
photo_kind / photo_source_url / photo_reuse_status: attribution and reuse status.
Photo reuse permission has not been established. Blank portraits were not invented.
contact_source_urls: evidence for the contact route.
activity_evidence_url: evidence for the work or role.
source_urls: sources supporting the record; some are historical or search-indexed.
source_date: publication, programme or relevant source date when available.
checked_on: research date, not a guarantee that a source is up to date.
verification_status: what was checked and what remains unconfirmed.
volunteer_status / volunteer_url / how_to_help: participation details and limits.
referral_route: how to request an introduction when direct contact is unavailable.
related_record_ids: links to related rows.
notes: conflicts, stale sources, interpretation and other material qualifications.
